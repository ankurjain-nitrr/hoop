# Quick questions

For a decision that blocks Claude's next step and fits in one line, Claude asks a quick question
instead of writing a document. It gives you the link in chat, with the question itself.

## Answer

A quick question holds one to four questions, each with two to four options and one marked
recommended.

- **One click** picks an option. It is saved and sent to Claude at once.
- **Other** opens a box for your own words; Enter sends them.
- **A note** under a question goes with its answer.
- **Go with your recommendation** answers every question still open with Claude's picks.

Nothing ever answers for you on a timeout: only you answer.

## Change an answer

Pick again any time. Once Claude has read the first answer, the new one reaches Claude as a
correction, and the question keeps both, with the earlier ones folded under **Earlier answers**.

## Beside a review line

A question about a line in an open review also shows as a card beside that line on the review's
page. Answering there sends only the answer: it doesn't start or finish your review of the
document, and the answered card stays on its line. While the review is open, a new card shows
without a reload.

## When it ends

- **Withdrawn.** Claude can withdraw a question it no longer needs, with a one-line reason, which
  the page shows.
- **Closed.** **Close** ends a question you don't want to answer, without approving it. An answer
  afterwards reopens it.

Open quick questions show on [Home](home.md) under Pending.
